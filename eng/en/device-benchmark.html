💡 The Key Insight

As an API-based AI workstation, the S21 has almost no performance bottleneck.

Claude Code, GitHub, YouTube, and Telegram are all API calls. What matters is network bandwidth, not local compute power. The real limitation of this 5-year-old phone is battery life (4,000mAh after 5 years of use) and the inability to run LLMs locally (no NPU). But in our cloud-API architecture, neither is a problem.

Why a 5-year-old phone can do modern AI development:
All heavy computation is offloaded to cloud APIs. The phone is just a thin client with a terminal — and for that, the S21 is more than enough.
